Maize Production: From Seed to Harvest
Unit 01: Crop Production Certified Seed (Dale Eagle Seeds) Commercial Grain

Commercial maize cultivation at Soardale Resources is engineered for scale, genetic purity, and consistent grain yield. In partnership with our licensed subsidiary, Dale Eagle Seeds Ltd, we cultivate both high-yield certified seed varieties and bulk commercial maize for human consumption, industrial milling, and livestock feed compounding.

Agronomic Lifecycle & Best Practices

  • Variety Selection: High-yielding, drought-tolerant, and disease-resistant hybrid and open-pollinated varieties (SAMMAZ series).
  • Land Preparation: Deep disc plowing, harrowing, and precision ridging to maximize root penetration and moisture conservation.
  • Nutrient Management: Basal NPK fertilizer application followed by targeted urea side-dressing during active vegetative growth.
  • Pest & Fall Armyworm Defense: Proactive integrated pest management (IPM) with biological controls and scheduled scouting.
  • Harvesting & Conditioning: Cobs harvested at physiological maturity, mechanically dehusked, and conditioned down to ≤12.5% moisture for long-term safe storage.

Commercial Supply & Offtake

  • Certified seed maize in tagged, tamper-proof bags for farmers and commercial outgrowers
  • High-grade yellow and white maize grain for feed millers and food processors
  • Bulk supply with certified moisture, purity, and germination ratings

Paddy Rice Cultivation & Precision Milling
Unit 01: Crop Production Lowland & Swamp Ecology Stone-Free Table Rice

Our paddy rice operations combine sustainable wetland soil stewardship with modern water management and post-harvest milling. We cultivate proven high-yield FARO cultivars that deliver exceptional tillering capacity, uniform grain filling, and outstanding culinary quality.

Cultivation & Field Operations

  • Nursery & Transplanting: Raised-bed nursery management followed by precision 20cm × 20cm hill transplanting into puddled, leveled paddies.
  • Water Control: Controlled bunding and drainage systems ensuring optimum water depth during tillering and panicle initiation.
  • Weed & Avian Management: Integrated weed suppression and continuous bird monitoring during the milky-to-dough grain stage.
  • Harvesting: Timely sickling and mechanical threshing at 20-22% moisture to prevent grain cracking and sun-checking.

Milling & Market Readiness

  • Hot-water soaking and controlled steam parboiling to lock in vital nutrients within the grain
  • Multi-pass de-stoning, de-husking, polishing, and optical grading
  • 100% stone-free, whole-grain table rice and broken rice for brewing and animal feeds

Soybean Farming: Feed Grade & Industrial Oil
Unit 01: Crop Production High-Protein Legume Soil Nitrogen Fixer

Soybeans play a central double role at Soardale Resources: providing high-protein raw material for the commercial animal feed and edible oil industries while replenishing soil nitrogen through biological fixation as part of our sustainable crop rotation strategy.

Agronomy & Crop Management

  • Inoculation & Seeding: Seed treatment with Bradyrhizobium japonicum for vigorous root nodulation and nitrogen fixation.
  • Bed Preparation: Well-drained, friable seedbeds planted in optimum moisture windows to guarantee high emergence rates.
  • Canopy Development: Rapid early-season weed control allowing dense canopy closure to conserve soil moisture and naturally shade out weeds.
  • Harvest Timing: Strict monitoring of pod color and moisture (13-14%) to harvest before pod shattering occurs.

Industrial Applications & Products

  • High-protein clean soybean grain for poultry and livestock feed compounding
  • High-oil content beans supplied for industrial solvent extraction and vegetable oil refining
  • Soybean cake and meal by-products for commercial livestock rations

Commercial Cassava Cultivation & Processing
Unit 01: Crop Production High-Starch TME Cultivars Value Addition

Cassava is a resilient pillar of our crop unit. We grow improved high-tonnage, disease-resistant stems and link harvesting directly to our on-farm and partner processing hubs to prevent post-harvest physiological deterioration and extract maximum value.

Production & Agronomic Protocols

  • Certified Stem Cuttings: 20–25cm mature, pest-free stem cuttings planted angled or horizontal on structured mounds and ridges.
  • Weed Management: Strict weed-free maintenance during the critical first 90 days until full leaf canopy establishment.
  • Extended Growth Cycle: 10–12 month maturation ensuring maximum tuber dry-matter content and heavy yield per hectare (25–35 t/ha).
  • Selective Harvest: Mechanical and manual uprooting timed to coincide with immediate same-day processing.

Processed Derivatives & Value Chains

  • High-Quality Cassava Flour (HQCF) for industrial bakery and confectionary blending
  • Hygienically processed, crisp, and stone-free table garri and fufu mash
  • Sun-dried and kiln-cured cassava chips and peel mash for livestock feed

Sheep & Goat Rearing & Intensive Fattening
Unit 02: Animal Production Small Ruminants Ram Fattening Schemes

Soardale's small ruminant unit blends semi-intensive pasture browsing with formulated feedlot rations. We specialize in rearing resilient indigenous breeds and executing targeted ram fattening programs for festive and commercial culinary markets.

Breeding & Management Standards

  • Adapted Breeds: Red Sokoto, Sahelian, and West African Dwarf goats; Balami, Uda, and Yankasa sheep crosses.

Commercial Offerings

Intensive Catfish Aquaculture & Processing
Unit 03: Aquaculture Clarias Gariepinus Fresh & Oven-Smoked

Our aquaculture division leverages deep borehole freshwater, earthen grow-out ponds, and concrete nursing tanks to rear fast-growing African catfish (Clarias gariepinus) under stringent water quality, feeding, and biosecurity regimes.

Aquaculture Operations & Quality Control

  • Hatchery & Nursery: High-survival fingerling and juvenile production from select broodstock with regular bi-weekly size grading to eliminate cannibalism.
  • Water Quality Dynamics: Continuous monitoring of dissolved oxygen (DO), temperature, and pH with systematic pond flushing and solar aeration.
  • Nutritional Precision: Feeding premium extruded floating feed (42–45% crude protein) calibrated to body weight for maximum feed conversion ratio.
  • Kiln Processing: Value-added smoking in stainless steel, temperature-regulated ovens yielding aromatic, hygienic, and sand-free smoked catfish.

Commercial Offerings

  • Live table-size catfish (1kg, 1.5kg, 2kg+) for hotels, restaurants, and fish merchants
  • Robust, disease-resistant juveniles (6–8cm) for commercial grow-out operators
  • Vacuum-sealed, kiln-smoked catfish with extended shelf life and zero chemical additives

Sustainable Tilapia Farming & Polyculture
Unit 03: Aquaculture Oreochromis Niloticus Fresh Table Fish

We farm Nile Tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) in dedicated earthen and semi-intensive concrete ponds. By prioritizing clean running water, plankton-rich pond ecology, and balanced floating rations, we produce sweet, firm-fleshed table tilapia.

Pond Culture & Sustainable Practices

  • Monosex Stocking: Stocking 100% all-male monosex fingerlings to prevent uncontrolled pond reproduction and ensure rapid, uniform growth rates.
  • Plankton & Feed Management: Controlled organic pond manuring to promote natural phytoplankton bloom combined with high-protein supplementary feeds.
  • Oxygenation: Surface aeration and freshwater exchange to support clean breathing and optimal fish vigor.
  • Careful Harvest: Seine netting at dawn with immediate cold-chain icing to preserve pristine freshness and texture.

Commercial Offerings

  • Live and chilled table-size whole tilapia for seafood markets, retailers, and restaurants
  • Quality monosex tilapia fingerlings for aquaculture farms and polyculture systems

Pure Raw Honey Extraction & Hive By-Products
Unit 04: Apiary & Honey 100% Unadulterated Cold-Filtered • Raw

We harvest and bottle 100% pure, raw, unadulterated multi-flora honey and natural hive by-products. Our cold-processing technique ensures that every jar retains its natural enzymes, floral aroma, rich antioxidants, and wholesome pollen content.

Harvesting & Processing Excellence

  • Capped Comb Harvesting: Harvesting only 100% sealed, fully ripe honeycombs to guarantee moisture levels below 18% for natural shelf stability.
  • Cold Extraction: Uncapping and centrifugal extraction performed strictly without heat treatment or pasteurization to preserve living enzymes.
  • Gravity Micro-Filtration: Stainless steel double-mesh straining that removes wax particles while retaining microscopic bee pollen and propolis traces.
  • Zero Additives: Certified 100% free of corn syrup, added sugars, artificial colors, or chemical preservatives.

Products & Packaging Formats

  • Pure raw bottled honey in food-grade glass and squeeze jars (350ml, 500ml, 1 Litre, and 25-Litre bulk containers)
  • Clean, cosmetic and pharmaceutical-grade pure beeswax blocks for body care and polish makers
  • Raw propolis and natural bee pollen for wellness and therapeutic applications
