What we produce

Four units. One farm.

Crop production, livestock, aquaculture and apiary work — each run by people who know the difference between a good season and a lucky one.

Unit 01

Crop Production

Field crops grown for scale and consistency — from land preparation through to post-harvest handling, with an eye on the varieties that perform best in our soil and climate.

Maize

Grain and certified seed maize, grown for both household consumption and industrial buyers.

Paddy rice

Cultivated and processed through to milled rice ready for market.

Soya beans

Grown for food, feed, and industrial oil markets.

Cassava

Cultivated and processed into flour, starch and related products.

Unit 02

Animal Production & Processing

Housing, nutrition and health management built around each species — raised for meat, eggs and breeding stock, with biosecurity taken seriously at every pen.

Poultry

Layers for egg production, plus cockerel and broiler production for meat.

Piggery

Pork and bacon production, from weaning through to finishing.

Sheep & goats

Rearing and fattening for meat markets.

Small & large ruminants

Rabbit, grasscutter and cattle production.

Unit 03

Aquaculture Production

Pond-managed fish farming with close attention to water quality, feeding schedules and disease prevention — carried from fingerling to market-ready size.

Catfish

Raised in managed ponds through to market weight.

Tilapia

Farmed alongside catfish under the same water-management standards.

Unit 04

Apiary & Honey Production

Managed hives, tended for healthy colonies and a clean, pure harvest — extracted, filtered and bottled on site.

Apiary management

Hive construction, placement and colony health maintained year-round.

Honey production

Harvested, filtered and bottled honey, plus other hive products.

Consultancy

Talk to the people running the farm.
