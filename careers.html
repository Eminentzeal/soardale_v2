Driver
Full-time Abeokuta, Ogun State On-site

Keep our produce, inputs and people moving safely between the farm, our processing points and the market. You'll be trusted with the vehicle, the schedule and the delivery.

Responsibilities

  • Transport produce, livestock feed and farm inputs between sites
  • Run market and supplier errands as scheduled
  • Keep the vehicle clean, fuelled and maintained
  • Maintain accurate trip and delivery logs
  • Follow all road safety and farm site procedures

Requirements

  • Valid driver's licence with a clean record
  • Minimum 2 years commercial or farm driving experience
  • Familiarity with Abeokuta and surrounding routes
  • Basic vehicle maintenance knowledge

Agronomist
Full-time Abeokuta, Ogun State On-site

Own the cultivation cycle for our maize, rice, soya bean and cassava fields — from soil prep to harvest — with an eye on yield, seed quality and sustainable practice.

Responsibilities

  • Plan planting, fertilisation and harvest schedules by crop and season
  • Monitor crop health, soil condition and pest/disease pressure
  • Advise on seed selection, especially for certified seed maize
  • Track yield data and recommend improvements season to season
  • Coordinate with field labour and the production manager

Requirements

  • Degree or diploma in Agronomy, Crop Science or related field
  • Practical field experience with staple crops in Nigeria
  • Comfortable with hands-on, on-site work through the season
  • Basic record-keeping and reporting skills

Marketers
Full-time Ogun State Field-based

Carry our crop, livestock, fish and honey products to the buyers who need them — building distributor relationships and growing household demand.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and build relationships with distributors, retailers and bulk buyers
  • Represent Soardale's full product range across units
  • Meet agreed sales targets and report on pipeline progress
  • Gather market feedback to guide production planning
  • Represent the brand at markets, fairs and partner visits

Requirements

  • Experience in sales, marketing or distribution, ideally agro-products
  • Strong communication and negotiation skills
  • Willingness to travel within Ogun State and beyond
  • Self-motivated, target-driven approach

Farm Technician
Full-time Abeokuta, Ogun State On-site

Provide daily, hands-on care across our livestock, poultry and pond units — the kind of steady attention that keeps animals healthy and production on track.

Responsibilities

  • Feed and monitor livestock, poultry and fish stock on schedule
  • Watch for signs of illness and report to the production manager promptly
  • Maintain clean, safe pens, coops and ponds
  • Keep accurate feeding, health and stock records
  • Support vaccination, weighing and restocking exercises

Requirements

  • Prior experience with livestock, poultry or aquaculture care
  • Comfortable with early hours and physical, outdoor work
  • Basic literacy for record-keeping
  • Reliable and detail-oriented
