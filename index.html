What we do

Four units, one standard of care.

Each unit runs to its own discipline — but every one of them answers to the same commitment: quality product, honestly produced.

01

Crop Production

Maize, paddy rice, soya beans and cassava, grown and processed for household and industrial buyers.

02

Livestock

Layers, broilers, piggery, sheep, goats and small ruminants — raised for meat, eggs and breeding stock.

03

Aquaculture

Pond-managed catfish and tilapia, from fingerling to market-ready size.

04

Apiary & Honey

Managed hives producing pure honey and other hive products, harvested and bottled on site.
