About Soardale

From a CAC certificate to a working farm.

An agro-allied and food company built on animal rearing, crop production and food processing with each stage grown deliberately, one registration and one hive at a time.

Who we are

Agriculture, taken as a full system.

Soardale Resources Agro-Allied and Food Company is an agricultural operating business that bases its operation on animal rearing, crop production and agricultural food processing. We don't treat these as separate businesses, they're stages of the same commitment: getting good food from land to household.

Our seed subsidiary, Dale Eagle Seed Ltd, carries that same standard into certified maize seed, licensed by Nigeria's National Agricultural Seed Council.

Vision

A trusted household name.

Making quality products available at affordable prices, building a name that's trusted in our immediate and distant environments, and across the wider business arena.

Mission

Protein at a fair price.

Giving every household easy access to proteinous food at an affordable price and creating jobs for unemployed graduates along the way. We call it empowering farmers, with quality.
